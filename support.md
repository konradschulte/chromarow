# Chromarow Support

Chromarow is a color-route dice strategy game for two to five players. You can play locally, in an online room, or with nearby iPhones without internet access.

## Common questions

### Do I need an account?

No. Chromarow does not require an account, login, subscription, or in-app purchase.

### How do online rooms work?

One player creates a private room and shares its invitation link, QR code, or six-character code. Other players join from Chromarow or a supported browser. The host starts the game after all seats are filled. Online rooms require internet access.

If you leave and return, use the same device and room invitation where possible. A host who explicitly leaves closes the room for everyone. Inactive rooms expire automatically after approximately 12 hours.

### How does nearby offline play work?

Enable Bluetooth, Wi-Fi, and Local Network access on every participating iPhone. One player creates a nearby game and the other players select that host from the Nearby screen. Internet access and a Wi-Fi router are not required, but both radios must remain enabled.

Keep Chromarow open on all devices. The host must stay connected. Nearby play cannot currently migrate a match to a new host if the original host closes the app or disconnects permanently.

### Why does Chromarow request motion access?

Motion access enables the optional shake-to-roll feature. You can decline it and always use the on-screen roll control instead. Raw motion measurements are not stored or uploaded.

### Why does Chromarow request Bluetooth and Local Network access?

These permissions are used to discover and connect nearby iPhones for offline multiplayer. They are not required for a local single-device game.

### Why did an invitation open the website instead of the app?

Ensure the App Store version of Chromarow is installed, then open the invitation from Messages, Mail, Notes, or another app. iOS may keep a same-domain link in Safari when it is tapped from a Chromarow webpage. You can also enter the room code manually.

### What should I try if a game stops responding?

For an online room, confirm that the internet connection is available and reopen the invitation or enter the room code again. For nearby play, keep both Wi-Fi and Bluetooth enabled, bring the devices closer together, and reconnect to the same host. If the host has ended a nearby session, a new game must be created.

### Is my music supposed to continue?

Chromarow is configured to mix game sounds with other audio. You can also disable game sound or select another sound theme in Settings.
